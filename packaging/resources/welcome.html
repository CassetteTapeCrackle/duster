Duster

A kick synth. Play it with MIDI and it makes the whole kick, with a ringy, noisy top end crushed out of the kick itself.

This installs the Audio Unit and VST3 versions for all users:

  • /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Duster.component
  • /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Duster.vst3

Click Customize on the next step to install only one of them. Requires macOS 12 or later, Apple silicon or Intel.
